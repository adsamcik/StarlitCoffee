Description

Pulsar Benefits

Flow Control

The Pulsar brewer offers complete control over agitation and flow rate, and makes it easy to retain water during the blooming phase.

Diffused Pouring

Start a controlled brew with a uniform wetting of the grounds with the carefully designed dispersion cap.

High Versatility

The valve and the "no bypass" brewer design provide an unlimited number of possible combinations of immersion and percolation.

No Water Bypass

Eliminate the unknown amount of water bypassing the coffee bed (Easily control the strength with brew ratio)

Heat Retention

The combination of thermal stable plastic and dispersion cap give this brewer the advantage of higher slurry temps.

Tea Brewing

The ideal dripper for steep & release tea brewing. Gong-fu tea brewing is a traditional Chinese method of making tea.

Steep-and-Release

Perfect for simple brews 'steep-and-release' brews.

  18. Rated 5 out of 5

    Ben Shields – August 11, 2026

    Wonderful brewer! I first came across this at Seattle Coffee Fest 2025. 9 months later I’m finally leaving a review!

    This brewer’s features combine in a pretty unique way: the no-bypass design of Aeropress with even more consistency than a Hario Switch, and very easy to travel with. And the valve feels like a unique mechanism to play with flow rate in a way somewhat isolated from grind size.

    I find myself reaching for this brewer when I have a bag of washed coffee: especially anything with notes of chocolate, but even floral tea-like beans. I enjoy expressive light roasts, and occasionally in the medium roast realm.

    I still haven’t explored all of the ways to use this brewer, but I’m happy to continue tinkering for years to come (bloom, agitation, water recipes and temp, optional bypass, flow rate via valve, pour structure, on and on). I personally haven’t dialed in wild coffees on this brewer yet but I’m sure it’s doable (highly processed, anaerobic, co-ferment, etc), but I’m so delighted with the clean rich delicious chocolatey cups I can make with the Pulsar with easy consistency! 😀

    For what it’s worth, my two cents are to brew to your own tastes instead of copying recipes, and that I have a lot of success with consistency and avoiding clogging by grinding coarser than V60 and then doing a quick blind shaker step to make an even bed, with no to minimal agitation. (I should revisit trying ‘water first + WWDT/stir’ instead of blind shaker).

    For me, the Next Level Pulsar finds regular use among my brewers: Aeropress, plastic V60, Hario Switch, OXO Rapid Brewer. I think they can all be used to make very similar coffee (other than the ORB), but they all have different workflows and strengths that lend themselves to more consistently hitting certain types of cups. I love each and every one of them, but I think the Pulsar might be the most versatile.
