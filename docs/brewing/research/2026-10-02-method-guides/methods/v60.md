# Hario V60: a first hot cup

Editorially reviewed, 2026-10-02. English research content; no physical brew test.

The V60 drains water through coffee and a cone paper filter. This guide covers one hot cup in a conventional plastic V60 02. It operationalizes [Hario UK's September 2026 recipe](https://www.hario.co.uk/blogs/news/how-to-brew-coffee-with-hario-v60-coffee-dripper), choosing 93 °C, a 30-second wait after the bloom pour, and a total clock starting at first water. These choices are app-authored.

## Equipment and starting recipe

Use matching **02 V60 cone paper**, a burr grinder, controllable kettle, scale, timer, and stable heat-resistant mug or server. Hario labels sizes [01: 1–2 cups](https://www.hario-usa.com/products/vd-01t), [02: 1–4 cups](https://www.hario-usa.com/products/v60-plastic-coffee-dripper-02), and [03: 1–6 cups](https://www.hario-usa.com/products/v60-plastic-dripper-03). These labels do not define a universal mug volume or water-fill capacity. Match filter and dripper sizes.

Start with **15 g coffee, 250 g brew water, 93 °C**, and a medium grind resembling granulated sugar: **1:16.67 by input mass**. Have extra rinse water, which is discarded. There is no ice or dilution, and no promised beverage yield.

## Steps

1. **Prepare.** Fold the paper seam, fit and rinse with hot water, then empty the receiving vessel. This wets the paper and warms the equipment. Check stable support.
2. **Dose.** Add coffee, level it, and zero the entire assembly on the scale. Keep that zero; later targets include earlier water.
3. **Bloom.** Start the timer at first water. Pour **45 g**, wetting the whole bed. Then wait **30 seconds** after reaching 45 g, not until a fixed 0:30 timestamp. Bubbles may appear; a quiet bloom alone does not indicate failure.
4. **Pour.** Add **105 g** in controlled circles over the grounds to **150 g cumulative**. Pause until the liquid level visibly falls; no fixed pause duration is specified. Add **100 g** to reach **250 g cumulative**. Keep liquid below the rim. Do not add separate 45/150/250 g portions.
5. **Finish.** Let standing water drain and the continuous stream become occasional drops. Remove by the hot brewer's handle and mix the coffee gently. The [selected recipe](https://www.hario.co.uk/blogs/news/how-to-brew-coffee-with-hario-v60-coffee-dripper) suggests **2:30–3:00 overall**, including bloom. Actual drainage and taste take priority over that reference time.

## When the cup needs adjustment

Check paper size and seating, clear outlet, receiver airflow, discarded rinse water and scale zero. Paper changes can alter timing; [Hario distinguishes faster-flow and classic filters](https://www.hario-usa.com/products/v60-meteor-filters-02).

If a fast cup tastes thin or sour, check edge pouring and bed level, then try slightly finer grinding. If drainage stays slow and taste is harsh or drying, try slightly coarser grinding after equipment checks. Change one variable while keeping other inputs consistent. Hario's taste adjustments are starting suggestions, not diagnoses from time alone. Decaf does not justify a universal shorter finish.

## Other recipes and care

[Rao's interview](https://www.hario.co.uk/blogs/hario-ambassadors/hario-v60-recipe-interview-with-hario-ambassador-scott-rao) uses plastic, 20 g coffee, 97 °C, aggressive bloom spin, two main pours and gentle spins. Original cumulative targets are 60/200/330 **mL**; usual total is 4:00–4:30. Volumes are not exact gram measurements.

[Kasuya's interview](https://www.hario.co.uk/blogs/hario-ambassadors/hario-v60-recipe-interview-with-hario-ambassador-tetsu-kasuya) gives 20 g coarse coffee, 300 mL at 92 °C; additions 50/70/60/60/60 mL at 0:00/0:45/1:30/2:10/2:40; removal at 3:30. His modified ceramic model slows flow. Identify the brewer; his taste framework is not a universal extraction law.

[Kurasu's flash recipe](https://kurasu.kyoto/blogs/recipe/japanese-brew-guide-on-iced-pour-over-coffee) is separate: 16 g medium-fine coffee, 150 g hot water at 91 °C and 70 g ice in the server. Hot additions are 40/60/50 g at 0:00/0:40/1:10, each approximately ten seconds. Allow drawdown and melt the original brew ice before serving over fresh ice. Neither 150 g nor 220 g is a promised beverage yield.

Keep hands clear of hot water; put the removed brewer on a heat-resistant surface. Cool, discard grounds and paper, wash gently, rinse and dry. Check model instructions before dishwasher or heating use. Revalidate grind, pouring and drainage after batch, material or filter changes.
