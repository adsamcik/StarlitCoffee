# Learning-guide factual audit — 2026-08-19

## Scope and method

This audit covers the 20 exact P1 guides plus the NextLevel Pulsar guide shown
in Learn. Dose, water, ice, ratio, temperature, stage timing, cumulative pours,
equipment state, and completion cues were compared with the named original or
manufacturer source where one exists. Fixed coffee-to-water ratios were also
recomputed from the stored quantities.

## Result

- One source contradiction was found and corrected: the Clever water-first
  guide released at 2:00. Hoffmann's technique breaks the crust at 2:00, waits
  another 30 seconds, releases at 2:30, and expects roughly one minute of
  drawdown.
- All fixed ratios recompute correctly from the stored dose and input-water
  values. The Kurasu flash guide correctly distinguishes its 1:9.38 hot-water
  ratio from its 1:13.75 total water-plus-ice ratio.
- The Chemex, generic conical, repeated-rise cezve, and screw-phin entries remain
  explicitly described as baselines or lower-confidence variants. They are not
  presented as exact official or creator recipes.
- No contradiction was found in the Pulsar guide: 20 g coffee, 340 g total
  water, a 60 g closed-valve bloom, release at 1:00, approximately 99 °C water,
  and a 3:30–4:30 finish match Gagné's published technique.

## Implementation note

The 2026-07-27 evidence projection is immutable provenance. The Clever
correction is therefore applied as a reviewed runtime erratum to every locale,
while the typed stage plan and recipe-level expected time use the corrected
values directly. This keeps historical evidence identity intact and prevents
stale translated numbers from reaching users.
