# P1 exact-stage illustration blocker resolutions

Date: 2026-08-03

These decisions bound illustration hardware only. They do not overwrite the
canonical recipe quantities, timings, evidence class, unresolved grinder
fields, or stable recipe and stage identifiers. A different physical
configuration must not reuse these exact illustrations without its own review.

## `BLOCK-V60-VARIANT`

Use the matte-black ceramic Hario V60 02 Kasuya Model (`KDC-02-B`) with one
matching white 02 conical paper and a clear server for both affected recipes.

- Hario identifies `KDC-02-B` as the 1–4 cup Kasuya-series dripper developed
  for the 4:6 method.
- Kurasu's exact 16 g / 150 g hot water / 70 g ice recipe identifies the Hario
  V60 Dripper 02 Tetsu Kasuya Model and matching V60 paper.
- This is a visual/equipment qualification. It does not claim that every V60
  material or size produces identical flow.

Sources:

- https://global.hario.com/pdf2019/2019HARIO_GC_COFFEE_EN.pdf
- https://kurasu.kyoto/blogs/kurasu-journal/brew-guide-on-iced-pour-over-coffee
- https://jp.kurasu.kyoto/blogs/kurasu-journal/iced-pourover-coffee-hariov60

## `BLOCK-WEDGE-VARIANT`

Use the black Melitta 1-Cup Pour-Over cone with one folded white Melitta #2
paper. Do not combine #2 and #4 geometry in one frame.

Melitta states that this brewer accepts #2 or #4 cone papers and ships with #2
papers. Selecting the supplied #2 option creates one source-supported exact
configuration while preserving the source's unresolved alternative.

Source:

- https://shoponline.melitta.com/products/1-cup-pour-over-coffee-brew-cone-black

## `BLOCK-GENERIC-CONE`

Use one neutral, unbranded reference configuration defined by the app:

- size-02-class white conical paper;
- a 60-degree open-bottom cone;
- one clearly open circular apex outlet;
- restrained neutral ribs shown only as physical supports;
- no logo, copied product silhouette, or filter-performance claim.

This illustration is explicitly schematic. It teaches the source-faithful bed,
pour, and drawdown state, not universal compatibility or equivalent flow. The
generic recipe remains ineligible for any real brewer whose paper fit, outlet,
ribs, capacity, or bypass path has not been confirmed separately.

Canonical evidence remains `SRC-HARIO-V60-OFFICIAL`, `SRC-SCHMIEDER-FLOW`, and
`SRC-COFFEEADASTRA-FINES`; this decision adds no inferred performance claim.

## `BLOCK-CEZVE-HARDWARE`

Use the tin-lined, hand-hammered copper STC Pro family:

- STC Pro 1, 150 ml full capacity, for the 65 g one-cup recipe;
- STC Pro 2, 240 ml full capacity, for the 130 g two-cup recipe;
- the long heat-resistant alloy handle and dual spouts remain visible;
- heating frames use a stable small gas grate with a low flame contained under
  the pot base; the handle stays outside the flame envelope.

The selected capacities leave visible foam headroom. The illustration must not
teach a fill-to-rim state, a rolling boil, unattended heating, or a bare hand on
hot metal. UNESCO and Mehmet Efendi remain authoritative for cold water, fine
coffee, slow heating, foam, and small-cup service. The SCA product record and
manufacturer specifications bound modern pot geometry and material.

## `BLOCK-AUTO-BATCH-HARDWARE`

Use the Moccamaster KBGV Select 1.25 L glass-carafe configuration for both
automatic-batch recipes:

- one No. 4 cone paper in the matching cone basket;
- the attached nine-hole outlet arm;
- the glass carafe seated on the hotplate;
- half-volume selection for 500 g and full-volume selection for 1,000 g;
- hands remain clear of the hot outlet arm during and after brewing.

Moccamaster's KBGV Select guide specifies the No. 4 paper, nine-hole arm, glass
carafe, and 4–6 versus 8–10 cup selector. This resolution does not transfer the
art to flat baskets, thermal carafes, manual drip stops, or other machines.
