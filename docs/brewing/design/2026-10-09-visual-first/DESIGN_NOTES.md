## Text that still earns its place

- Keep cumulative totals, beverage-yield labels and explicit long-duration units. A recipe target is not a sensor reading.
- Keep first drips, all grounds wet, the black-base grip, clean server, filter rim and high resistance explicit when relevant. A picture alone can lose these conditions.
- Keep brief actionable safety words: Hot · lift only by base; Covered · ≤4 °C. Icons support them.
- Label diagrams Target or End cue, not confirmed measurements. The 1 cm marker measures from the top of the coffee bed to the target waterline. Drawdown has no distance bracket.
- Keep pending/failed reports distinct from repeating physical work, unknown clocks unknown, and background sessions independent. Pause guide does not pause extraction.

The existing bloom identity remains before/during bloom and in its final pose afterward. It is not a second instruction diagram or proof that a pour finished. Methods without bloom omit it. Generated poses and equipment sketches are visual approximations; production must reuse the authoritative atlas and reviewed asset workflow.
