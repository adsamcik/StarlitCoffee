# Czech exact-guidance terminology

Status: editorial reference for the unapproved Czech draft. This document does
not authorize production coverage.

## Preferred vocabulary

| Canonical concept | Preferred Czech | Avoid | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| brewer / dripper | `dripper`, or the exact product name | `sládek`; a generic `zařízení na vaření kávy` in concise instructions | Czech specialty-coffee instructions commonly retain `dripper`; product names are clearer still. |
| coffee bed | `kávové lože` | `postel`; repeated `vrstva mleté kávy` where `kávové lože` is clearer | Czech V60 guidance uses `kávové lože` for the prepared coffee layer. |
| level the bed | `zarovnejte kávové lože` or `vyrovnejte povrch kávy` | `udělejte vrstvu` | The action is flattening an existing dose, not creating an unspecified layer. |
| bloom | `předspaření`; action `předspařte` | `výkvět`; literal flower senses | `Předspaření` is concise and unambiguous. `Kvést` may appear in explanatory prose, but is less clear in safety-critical steps. |
| swirl / spin | `jemně zakružte` | literal rotation machinery language | The instruction concerns a short circular hand motion of the brewer or carafe. |
| drawdown | `dokapání`; action `nechte kávu dokapat` | `odvodnění`; generic drainage engineering terms | The user observes the coffee finishing through the filter. |
| steep / immersion | `nechte louhovat`; `imerzní příprava` where a category is needed | `strmé` | `Steep` is a verb here, never the adjective “steep”. |
| server / carafe | `servírovací nádoba` or `karafa` | `server` in novice-facing instructions | Use the concrete vessel shown by the selected recipe. |
| grounds / fines | `mletá káva`, `sedlina`, or `jemné částice kávy` according to state | land, penalty, or fee senses | Dry coffee, settled sediment, and migrated fine particles are different concepts. |
| slurry | `směs kávy a vody` | an unexplained loanword | The plain description is immediately understandable. |
| Switch | `Switch` for the Hario model; `ventil Switche` for its valve | generic `spínač` when naming the brewer | The model name must not be translated into an electrical control. |
| filter paper | `papírový filtr` | bare `papír` where filter identity may be unclear | The exact compatible filter remains explicit. |
| heat-safe | `tepelně odolný` | `tepelně bezpečný` | This is the natural Czech material/safety property. |

## Style rules

- Use a direct imperative with an initial capital letter for every action.
- Preserve every canonical number token exactly, including the source's
  `1,000 g` notation and timer punctuation.
- Preserve observation-driven completion cues; do not turn them into blind
  timers.
- Keep `dose`, brew water, reservoir input, beverage yield, concentrate, ice,
  bypass, dilution, and final beverage distinct.
- Prefer a short concrete vessel or mechanism name over repeated machine-like
  phrases such as `zařízení na vaření kávy`.
- Preserve warning polarity and severity. Never soften `do not`, `never`, or a
  burn, pressure, overflow, flame, tipping, or electrical hazard.
- Keep brand and model names unchanged, including Hario V60, Hario Switch,
  Clever, Chemex, Kalita Wave, Cup-One, cezve/ibrik, and phin.

## Evidence used for the terminology pass

- Hario's Czech V60 material uses `papírové filtry`, `proplachujte`, and
  `extrakce kávy`.
- Czech specialty-coffee instructions use `dripper`, `papírový filtr`,
  `servírovací konvice` or `server`, circular pouring language, and
  `předspaření`/the established blooming concept.
- Czech V60 guidance also uses `kávové lože` and `vyrovnejte povrch` for a
  level bed.
- Czech Hario Switch guidance distinguishes a closed/open `ventil` and retains
  the Hario Switch product name.
